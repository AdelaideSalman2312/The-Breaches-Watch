Region/jurisdiction — Kenya/KDPA, EU/GDPR, US federal, etc.
Regulatory framework invoked — KDPA section, GDPR article, HIPAA, CMMC, NIS2, ISO 27001, EU AI Act, etc.

## Summary (As long as I find it necessary)

## Technical failure
What happened, mechanistically — the actual vulnerability/vector exploited.

## Detection & dwell time
How long between compromise and discovery, or between violation and
enforcement.

## Response
What the organization actually did.

## Governance failure
What internal control or policy should have caught or prevented this.

## Regulatory implication
Which law/framework applies; what enforcement (if any) followed;
penalty amount; whether it was appealed or upheld.

## Outcome
Cost, downtime, reputational/legal consequence.
